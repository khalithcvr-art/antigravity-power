# Expedia Services SEO Growth Plan

This is the operating plan for `https://expediaservices.ae`. It separates changes
we can ship in the repository from account actions that require the owner’s
Google access.

## Shipped technical foundation

- HTTPS, crawlable `robots.txt`, and an XML sitemap.
- English and Arabic canonical URLs with `en`, `ar`, and `x-default` hreflang.
- Prerendered English and Arabic service and jurisdiction pages.
- One visible H1 per page, accessible navigation, and reduced-motion support.
- Page-level `WebPage`/`Service` and `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD on prerendered pages.

## Priority keyword map

| Cluster | Primary intent | Page or content target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Abu Dhabi company formation | Commercial | Homepage and Abu Dhabi formation section |
| Dubai company formation | Commercial | `/mainland-business-setup` and Dubai guide |
| UAE PRO services | Commercial | PRO and government liaison service section |
| Abu Dhabi PRO services | Local commercial | Homepage contact and PRO section |
| Dubai PRO services | Local commercial | Dubai setup page and dedicated guide |
| UAE Golden Visa support | Commercial | Golden Visa service section and guide |
| UAE VAT and corporate tax registration | Commercial | Tax service section and tax guide |
| Meydan, IFZA, Masdar, Ajman company setup | Transactional | Existing jurisdiction pages |

Use the primary phrase once in the title and H1 where it reads naturally. Add
related terms through useful explanations, comparison tables, requirements,
authority links, and clear enquiry paths. Do not claim guaranteed approvals,
fixed government fees, or guaranteed rankings.

## Owner account actions

1. In Google Search Console, verify the domain property `expediaservices.ae`.
2. Submit `https://www.expediaservices.ae/sitemap.xml`.
3. Inspect the homepage, `/mainland-business-setup`, `/pro-services` if present,
   and each jurisdiction page; request indexing only when the rendered page is
   correct.
4. Claim or confirm the Google Business Profile. Keep the real business name,
   address, phone, hours, service area, and website identical to the website.
5. Add real office/team/service photographs and request honest reviews from
   completed customers. Never buy or incentivise reviews.
6. Add the business to relevant UAE directories and chambers using identical
   name, address, and phone (NAP) details.

## Measurement cadence

- Weekly: Search Console coverage, manual actions, and enquiry clicks.
- Monthly: impressions/clicks by query, top landing pages, GBP calls/directions,
  and review growth.
- Quarterly: refresh fee/legal references from official sources and expand the
  pages that receive impressions but few clicks.
